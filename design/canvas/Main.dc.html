Comic books with a lesson at heart

Build a katha the way the old storytellers did: choose the desh and kaal, bring in your paatra, set the paristhiti, and turn it into a comic your children can read, print and remember.

How it works

How a katha comes together

  1. 1

    Desh & Kaal

    Where and when: a forest by the Narmada, a kingdom in the Treta Yuga, a village at Diwali.

  2. 2

    Paatra

    The characters, human or animal, and what drives each one: dharma, artha, kaam or moksh.

  3. 3

    Paristhiti & Katha

    The situation, the plot and the lesson. Review every page before any picture is drawn.

  4. 4

    Read & print

    Single scenes for simple tales, six-panel comic pages for richer ones. Read aloud, print, or share.

Reference images are optional. They're only used to guide the drawings, never appear in shared links, and you can delete them at any time.
