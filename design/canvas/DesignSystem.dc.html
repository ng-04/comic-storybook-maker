Storybook Maker design system

The v1 comic look, tightened for a product: one ink, three loud colours on warm paper, a strict border and shadow scale, and Bangers reserved for things that should feel like a shout.

Shadows are always hard (no blur), offset down-right, in ink. Pressed state moves the element into its shadow. Radii stay small: 6 panels, 8 inputs, 10 buttons, pill chips.

Buttons and controls

VariantUse

Secondary (white)Back, regenerate, retry, all other actions.
BlueSharing and "draw sheet": actions that call an AI job but don't advance the flow.

Chips are toggle buttons with aria-pressed.

Focus: 4px blue outline, 3px offset, on every interactive element.

Page panel

One component draws every page everywhere: library covers, the progress grid, the editor, the reader and print. It scales with container units, so a thumbnail and an A4 sheet are the same markup. Text is always real HTML laid over the picture.

Purushartha and paatra icons

Each purushartha has a fixed colour, icon and name. The name is always shown, so the meaning never rests on colour alone.
